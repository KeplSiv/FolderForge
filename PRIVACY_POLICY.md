# Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 8, 2026

FolderForge does not collect, store, transmit, or share personal data.

## Data Collection

FolderForge does not:

- collect analytics
- use tracking
- send data to external servers
- require an account
- sell or share user data

## Local-Only Operation

FolderForge runs entirely on your Mac. Any folder icons, presets, backups, or related files are stored locally on your device and are never sent to us.

## Permissions

FolderForge may request macOS permissions such as file access or Finder automation only to perform app features you choose to use. Those permissions are used locally on your device.

## Third-Party Services

FolderForge does not use third-party analytics, advertising, or cloud processing services.

## Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, you can contact the developer through the FolderForge GitHub repository:
